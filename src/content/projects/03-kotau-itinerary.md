## Overview

A community-driven travel itinerary and discovery platform built with React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and Leaflet. Features interactive map-based exploration of local points of interest (POIs), custom route building, and exportable PDF travel itineraries with strict DOM sanitization.
